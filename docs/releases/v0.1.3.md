**Bring your own API key.** rdtnative comes without a Reddit API key: it only works with a
client ID you supply yourself.

### New

- **Easier sign-in:** the TV shows a short address (and a QR code) for a page it serves itself.
  Open it on a computer or phone, approve on Reddit, and paste the address Reddit sends you back
  to; pasting sends it to the TV at once. If your app's redirect URI points at the TV
  (`http://<TV IP>:8091/`), the TV catches it and there is nothing to copy.
- **Phones:** on Android, "Approve on Reddit" opens Chrome instead of the Reddit app; every phone
  gets a "Copy link" button to paste the link into the browser's address bar.
- **NSFW subreddits when signed out:** instead of Reddit's "Go to Reddit to view mature content"
  stand-in post, the feed says the subreddit needs a sign-in and offers "Sign in". Signed in to an
  account that hides mature content, it says where to turn it on.
- **Settings** says whether the sign-in survives a TV restart (it does unless the TV gives the
  app no lasting folder).

### Fixed

- **Pointer edge scrolling is smooth:** the page no longer jumps to a post's or comment's top
  while it glides; it stops wherever you leave the edge, half a post showing or not.
- A sign-in address from an earlier attempt is refused with a clear message.
- The sign-in page renews itself when it expires, and after a failed sign-in.

### Install

- **Homebrew Channel:** add the repository
  `https://github.com/danzig666/rdtnative/releases/latest/download/repo.json`.
- **By hand:** `ares-install --device <tv> io.github.danzig666.rdtnative_0.1.3_arm.ipk`.
