**Bring your own API key.** rdtnative comes without a Reddit API key: it only works with a
client ID you supply yourself.

### Signing in, made clear

How signing in goes depends on the redirect URI that comes with your client ID, and the TV's
sign-in screen now shows the steps for yours:

- **rdtnative's page** (`https://danzig666.github.io/rdtnative/callback`): nothing to copy.
  Choose "Sign in on this TV", or use a phone or computer: Reddit's answer comes back to the TV by
  itself.
- **An app address such as `myapp://…`**: **one copy and
  paste, on a computer.** Browsers can't open such an address and never hand it to a web page, so
  nothing can catch it by itself. This can't be avoided; you do it once per account, and the TV
  keeps the sign-in. On a computer, open the sign-in page the TV shows and press "Approve on
  Reddit". In Chrome or Edge, press Ctrl+Shift+J (Mac: Cmd+Option+J) in the Reddit tab before you
  press Allow, then copy the red "Failed to launch '…'" line (the whole line is fine; no such
  line? Press Allow again). In Firefox, copy the address bar after Allow. Switch back to the
  sign-in page and paste it there. Phones can't show this address.
- **Another web address:** copy the address the browser ends up on and paste it on the sign-in
  page (phone or computer). If you can change the redirect URI to rdtnative's page, nothing needs
  copying.

### Changed

- The sign-in screen speaks your language (Hungarian, German, Spanish, French, Russian).
