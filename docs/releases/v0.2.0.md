**Bring your own API key.** rdtnative comes without a Reddit API key: it only works with a
client ID you supply yourself. There are ways to get a working API key; search Reddit for them.

### Signing in

1. **Settings › Reddit API:** enter your client ID, with the remote or with "Enter on phone (QR
   code)". Leave the redirect URI empty to use rdtnative's own page; your Reddit app's redirect
   URI must then be `https://danzig666.github.io/rdtnative/callback`. If your client ID comes with
   another redirect URI, enter that one exactly. Press "Save and connect".
2. **Sign in** (side menu, or Settings › Sign in to Reddit), then one of:
   - **Sign in on this TV** (new): Reddit opens in the TV's browser. Log in with the remote and
     press Allow; the browser closes and rdtnative is signed in.
   - **A phone:** scan the QR code, press "Approve on Reddit", log in and press Allow.
   - **A computer** on the same network: type the short address the TV shows, then the same steps.

   With rdtnative's redirect page, Reddit's answer now goes back to the TV by itself, from any of
   the three: nothing to copy. With another redirect URI, copy the address the browser ends up on
   (it contains `code=`) and paste it into the phone or computer page. If that redirect URI is not
   a web address (like `myapp://…`), the browser shows nothing after Allow: use Firefox on a
   computer (the address bar holds it), or in Chrome or Edge press F12 › Console in Reddit's tab
   before Allow and paste the red "Failed to launch" line. Simplest is to set your Reddit app's
   redirect URI to rdtnative's page, if you can.
3. **More accounts:** Settings › Accounts › Add account.

### New

- **Lean back:** a **slideshow** or a **video channel** of any feed (the new Play button, or a
  subreddit's More menu): pictures change every few seconds, videos play to the end, Left/Right
  skip, OK pauses, Blue opens the comments. An optional **idle screen** shows pictures from a
  subreddit of your choice (Settings › Slideshow and idle screen). The TV's screensaver is held off
  while pictures or a video move.
- **Comments:** links to a comment (inbox replies, "Continue this thread", shared links) open on
  that comment. Comments new since your last visit are marked, and CH▲ / CH▼ jump between them;
  feeds show "+N" on posts with new comments. **Live mode** keeps match and game threads updating
  every 20 s. Comments can be saved (Yellow).
- **Player:** speed (0.5×–2×), quality (Auto to 360p), loop and replay, and the remote's Play,
  Pause, Stop, fast-forward and rewind keys.
- **Places:** custom feeds (multireddits), a subreddit info page (members, rules, sidebar, join)
  and user profiles (karma, cake day, posts and comments).
- **Accounts and inbox:** several accounts with a switcher; the inbox is checked every 2 minutes
  and a new message shows an alert; opened messages are marked read, long ones open in full. When
  Reddit stops taking the sign-in, the app says so and offers to sign in again.
- **Typing:** every keyboard can be typed on a phone (scan the QR code; the phone's microphone can
  dictate), replies keep their drafts, a USB keyboard types properly, and the colour buttons work
  in the keyboard. "Send to phone" in the hold-OK menu shows any post or comment as a QR code. LG
  voice search is tried where the TV lets the app use it.
- **Links** open in the TV's browser; comments show uploaded pictures and GIPHY stills.
- **Easier to use:** a line at the bottom shows what the colour buttons do; each feed keeps its
  place and the start page can be the last feed; Home is one button away from deep pages; failed
  pages can be retried with Down; errors are in plain words; sign out and other drastic actions
  ask first; first-time setup leads on to signing in.

### Fixed

- The colour buttons work on LG's own remote.
- BACK on the side menu closes it; BACK twice leaves the app (as in ytnative).
- Videos that the TV's player refuses as HLS play from the next stream; RedGIFs play again.
- Albums, previews and pictures too big for the TV's decoder use Reddit's resized copies instead
  of spinning forever.
- "Continue this thread" no longer opens the same page again.

### Install

- **Homebrew Channel:** add the repository
  `https://github.com/danzig666/rdtnative/releases/latest/download/repo.json`.
- **By hand:** `ares-install --device <tv> io.github.danzig666.rdtnative_0.2.0_arm.ipk`.
